Let's get stacking!

This is a from-scratch reimplementation of Tecmo Stackers (PS1, 1997). The game's art, sound and music come from your own copy of the disc.

  1. Have your disc as a BIN/CUE image of Tecmo Stackers (USA). Track 01 holds the art and sound; Tracks 02–12 hold the music.
  2. Click Choose disc files and select all the .bin files at once (Ctrl/⌘-A in the file picker).
  3. Press Enter to start. ? shows the controls.

Nothing is uploaded: the files are read here and saved in this browser, so next time the game starts straight away.

Press ? for controls · F fullscreen · M mute

Stackers Resto is a fan-made reimplementation, built for preservation and fun. No game data is included: it reads your own disc, in your browser. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Koei Tecmo.

Controls

Click a key to change it, then press the new key (Esc cancels). A key that's already in use swaps places.

    Playing

    ◀ ▶ move · ▼ drop faster · ✕ / ○ rotate · Start pause menu · △ back in menus.

    Chain Reaction

    □ drops a piece as blanks · L1/L2 zoom in/out · R1/R2 scroll up/down · △ next screen.

    Two players

    In a set match, □ sets off a bomb. Player 2 has their own keys (the Player 2 tab) or the second gamepad. During vs CPU, Player 2's Start brings in a challenger.

    Gamepads

    Plug in and press a button. D-pad or left stick · A/B = ✕/○ · X = □ · Y = △ · LB/RB = L1/R1 · LT/RT = L2/R2 · Start. With two pads connected, the second one is Player 2.

    Page keys

    M mute · P freeze · R restart · F fullscreen · ? this panel. These can't be rebound.
